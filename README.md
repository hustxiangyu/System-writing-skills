# System-writing-skills

> A practical paper-writing skill manual for the **computer systems** domain, distilled from the writing experience of faculty at Peking University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Nanjing University, and Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

[中文文档](./README.zh-CN.md) | [English Skills Manual](./SKILL.en.md) | [中文技能手册](./SKILL.zh-CN.md)

## What Is This

This repository packages a complete, battle-tested writing skill system for top-tier computer science papers (systems track). It covers:

- **Mental models** of academic writing: reviewer empathy, anticipating objections, explaining rather than describing
- **Section-by-section playbook**: Title, Abstract, Introduction, Background & Motivation, Design & Implementation, Evaluation, Related Work, Limitation & Conclusion
- **Sentence & paragraph craft**: claim-first paragraphs, 25-word sentence discipline, terminology consistency
- **Figure and layout standards**: figure-first communication, unified palette, typography rules
- **AI-era principles**: never let AI write your first draft; use reader-view self-check

The goal is simple: *make a reviewer understand what you did, why it matters, why it is hard, and why it works — in the shortest possible time.*

## Who Is It For

- Graduate students and researchers in computer systems (OS, architecture, networking, storage, distributed systems)
- Anyone preparing submissions to top systems venues (OSDI, SOSP, NSDI, EuroSys, ATC, FAST, ASPLOS, etc.)
- Authors who want a structured, actionable writing checklist instead of generic writing advice

## How to Use

1. Read `SKILL.zh-CN.md` (Chinese) or `SKILL.en.md` (English) end to end once — it is short and modular.
2. Keep the relevant module open while drafting each section of your paper.
3. Run the Module 4 self-check ("cache-flush re-read") before every submission.
